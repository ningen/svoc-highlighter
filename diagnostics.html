Diagnostics & settings

Collect real parser behavior locally, export it, and improve the gold-corpus regression suite.

Privacy: diagnostics stay in chrome.storage.local. Nothing is uploaded. Excluded hostnames are checked before DOM scanning or parsing starts.

Excluded hostnames

One JavaScript regular expression per line, matched case-insensitively against location.hostname only. Example: (^|\.)corp\.example\.com$. Lines beginning with # are comments.

Saving purges stored samples and host counters that now match. Matching sites skip DOM scanning, parsing, highlighting, and diagnostics on the next page load. Export filters samples again as a safety check. Exclusion patterns are not exported.

Analyzed hostnames

Hosts seen by the parser, ordered by processed sentence count. “Exclude” adds an exact-host regular expression and purges existing diagnostics for that host. Reload open pages for the exclusion to take effect.
